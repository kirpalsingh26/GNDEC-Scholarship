# 📖 User Walkthrough & Operational Guide
## **ScholarSphere — Step-by-Step Portals Manual**

---

### **1. Student Experience**
1. **Sign In**: Navigate to `http://localhost:5173/login` or click **👨‍🎓 Student** on the top demo switch bar.
2. **Dashboard**: View active scholarship progress, cumulative attendance gauge, and certified documents counter.
3. **Scholarship Discovery**: Open the **Scholarships** tab. Click **Check Rules & Criteria** to inspect the real-time explainable checklist.
4. **Apply**: Click **Apply Now**, review your academic & income snapshot, certify the declaration, and submit.
5. **Document Vault**: Upload income or caste certificates. Click **History** to view previous versions and review rejection remarks.
6. **Attendance & Recovery**: Test the **Interactive Recovery Simulator** by adjusting target percentages to view required consecutive classes.
7. **QR Pass**: Click **Digital Pass** in the navbar to render your cryptographic verification badge.

---

### **2. Scholarship Officer Experience**
1. **Sign In**: Click **🛡️ Officer** on the demo switch bar (`officer.rajesh@gndec.ac.in`).
2. **Needs Attention Triage**: Inspect high-priority shortages and pending documents from the main operations dashboard.
3. **Verification Workspace**: Open **Verification Workspace** -> select a document from the left queue -> inspect the preview and AI OCR extract in the center -> click **Verify ✓** or **Request Re-upload ↻** with remarks.
4. **Applications Review**: Open **Applications Queue** -> click **Review** on any pending application -> approve grant or request additional documents.
5. **Attendance Monitoring**: Access **Attendance Monitoring** to view sorted shortages and click **Broadcast Attendance Warnings**.

---

### **3. Super Administrator Experience**
1. **Sign In**: Click **⚙️ Admin** on the demo switch bar (`admin@gndec.ac.in`).
2. **Scheme Configuration**: Access **Scholarship Schemes** -> click **Add Scholarship Scheme** to configure grant amounts and threshold rules.
3. **User Management**: Modify staff permissions and activate/suspend accounts.
4. **Audit Logs**: Filter through real-time forensic activity logs.
5. **Report Exports**: Download Excel workbooks and CSV files under **Reports & Analytics**.
